# How the example comparison works

Sample terms from public policy wording — education only.

The scenario set places each confirmed condition at months 3, 18 and 30 (₹2,00,000 each), an accident at day 10 (₹1,00,000), and cataract at month 20 for a member aged 50 or older (₹60,000). Sliders change the example bill amounts. These are illustrative independent claims, not probabilities or a total for one policy year. Sum insured is assumed ₹5,00,000 per independent example; no bonus/restoration is modeled.

Code gates each claim against initial, pre-existing and specified-procedure waits, using the latest applicable cover date. Accidents bypass the initial illness wait. Care Instant Cover is selected and is a paid add-on for its named conditions; no Smart Select or Claim Shield is selected. Example non-medical items are assumed on the relevant covered list for HDFC Protect and Tata Consumables, and non-payable for Care. The assumed share is 8%; actual item eligibility must be checked. No voluntary deductibles or copays are assumed.

Sort by sum of modelled payouts descending; tie by fewer required add-ons, then plan label. All dates and amounts come from deterministic code and link to source clauses. Declaring a condition can change the premium; the insurer decides. Ask a licensed advisor for a quote. Premiums are not displayed.

Policy start is day 1. Initial cover starts at start + 30 days (day 31). Month/year additions use calendar months, including month-end clamping. Free-look deadline is receipt + 29 days; the day-12 reminder is receipt + 11 days. Dates are conservative simulations; confirm boundary dates with insurer/advisor. Continued coverage and insurer acceptance of declared conditions are assumed.
